### Lesson injection (ENGINEERING-LESSONS, sparse)
1. **Rollback 是有 blast radius 的變更，不是免費的還原鍵** — consumer 切換 A->B 期間 group A 停止消費；切回 A 就從停點重放，下游若沒有 idempotency gate 就是重複交付。任一方向切換前先把目標的 offset/queue 狀態推到當前，或明確接受重放；重啟會殺掉 in-flight job。-> the cutover runbook must state, per direction, what a peek-lock queue does to in-flight messages and whether the `storage-info-updates` handler is idempotent.
2. **安靜的觀測窗不是故障證據 — 要量輸入端** — cutover 後 N 分鐘零產出不能推論成壞了；先量 queue 的輸入（active message count / 上游 producer log），lag≈0 = 健康且輸入為空。-> the runbook's "verify within one cycle" step needs an input-side counter (SB active/dead-letter counts, LIS-DI-RESULT-V3 send log) next to the output-side check.
