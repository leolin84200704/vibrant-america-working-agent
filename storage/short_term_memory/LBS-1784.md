## Retrospective

### [2026-09-09]
- Baseline-first paid off: the NPI-twin (14738 / oc 1961) would have been silently deleted by an
  "OR NPI" bound DELETE. Binding the write to customer_id and only REPORTING NPI twins is the right shape
  for this system where NPI reuse across customer accounts is normal (6 accounts on one NPI).
- Time: ticket -> verified prod change in one session; the documented removal procedure + LBS-1773's
  script skeleton made this Routine.

## Lessons Learned
- NPI is not a customer identity in lis_core: bind prod writes to customer_id; use NPI only for
  discovery/reporting. Guard "exactly one row by customer OR NPI" is wrong for order_clients.
- Removal leaves result_enabled=1 on a REJECTED row — fine for the gate, but a future "reactivate" must
  not assume result_enabled reflects intent.
